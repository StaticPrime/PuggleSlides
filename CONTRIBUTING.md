# Contributing

Thank you for helping out! Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are all welcome.

## Reporting a bug or suggesting an idea

Open an issue on GitHub. For a bug, say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and include your macOS version. If you can, attach a file that shows the problem.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repository and make your change on a new branch.
2. Build the app and run the tests, as the README describes under *Building and running* and *Tests*.
3. Open a pull request that explains what the change does and why.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before your first pull request can be merged, you sign the [PuggleSoft Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). A bot asks you in the pull request, and signing takes one comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Every person who wrote a commit in the pull request signs once. The bot remembers your signature for later pull requests.

- You keep the copyright in your contribution.
- PuggleSoft can use, change and distribute your contribution. It can also license it under any terms, including licenses other than the GNU GPL and commercial licenses, without asking you again.
- You confirm that the work is yours to contribute. If your employer owns what you write, you need their permission.

Read [CLA.md](CLA.md) for the full terms. Contributions are accepted only under the agreement.
